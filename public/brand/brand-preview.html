TORCH MONO

A bespoke geometric monospace alphabet drawn as SVG paths on a 6px grid — cap height 72, advance 60, stem 12 — with a signature 1-unit 45° chamfer cut into every glyph's extreme top-left and bottom-right corner. It is the instrument-panel type of the product, redrawn as a wordmark rather than a font set on <text>.

The lockup is FIND:TIME. The colon is two lime dots — a digital-clock time separator that also echoes the StatusDot / traffic-light motif. Lime L-reticles lock onto TIME: the system has found an open slot and is blocking it. White glyphs on the electric-blue field; one-colour variants for every other surface.

Clear space & minimum size

clear space = one cap height (the F) on every side
  • Min width — horizontal lockup: 96px. Below that, switch to the icon.
  • Min size — icon: 16px.
  • Colon & reticles are always lime #CCFF00, never recoloured, except in the one-colour variants where the whole mark collapses to a single ink.
  • Do not outline the glyphs, add a drop shadow, re-space the letters, or place the white lockup on a light ground.

Why this mark

Find Time is a scheduling instrument, so the logo is built like one: a fixed monospace grid, hard geometry, a single repeated cut. The chamfer reads as a bevelled panel edge — the broadcast-console language of the product surface. The colon does double duty as a clock separator and a status light, and the reticles are the same L-shaped lime brackets the app uses to frame anything it has locked onto. The wordmark is not decoration applied to the product; it is the product's own drawing system at display size.
