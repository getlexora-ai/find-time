Where I'd land

Start with Deep Navy. It stays a “blue app,” so nothing about the brand has to be relearned, but it drops the saturation that made the original feel loud — and of the six it's the easiest to look at for a full working day.

If keeping the exact current hue matters — people have already seen the electric blue in the deck or on the site — Eclipse is that same colour at roughly a fifth of the brightness. Brand survives, volume drops.

Only go all the way to Ink if you want the app to read as strictly a focused utility, with no colour identity of its own. It makes the lime hit hardest, at the cost of feeling a little anonymous.

One build note for any dark ground: lift the #121212 panels to about #17181d with a 1px rgba(255,255,255,.12) border and a real shadow. On a near-black canvas they'd otherwise merge into it — the bright original is the only ground where #121212 separates on its own. Every other colour stays exactly as it is.
